Professional profile

Engineering depth with hands-on design leadership

A career spanning detailed engineering, 3D modelling, layout review, site execution support, customer coordination and multidisciplinary delivery.

Pritam Warghade is an experienced piping professional with strong expertise in piping layout development, pressure-drop calculation, flow-balance documentation, model review, design coordination and project delivery.

His work includes customer-facing technical coordination, preliminary engineering, P&ID and isometric review, utility-system calculations, support and nozzle-orientation checks, vendor coordination, site-oriented design validation and guidance to modelling and CAD teams.

He has delivered assignments for major engineering organizations in India, Oman and the UAE, with project exposure covering Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Nigeria, the United States, Russia and international nuclear infrastructure.
